# **Job Market & AI Impact Analysis (2024–2030)**
## **Overview**

This project analyzes global job market trends by comparing current (2024) and projected (2030) job openings across industries.
The focus is on understanding AI impact, automation risk, salary distribution, and experience requirements using an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The goal is to demonstrate end-to-end analytical thinking: from data understanding → insight generation → business-oriented conclusions.

## **Problem Statement**

How is AI-driven automation reshaping the global job market, and which industries and roles are expected to grow or decline by 2030?

## **Key Questions Answered**

Which industries will see the highest job growth by 2030?

How does AI impact level correlate with automation risk?

Which roles command higher median salaries?

What experience levels are most in demand across industries?

How does future job demand vary geographically?

## **Data**

Source: Public job market dataset
Granularity: Role-level, industry-level, country-level

## **Key Features**

Job Title, Industry, Country

Median Salary (USD)

Job Openings (2024, 2030)

Experience Required (Years)

AI Impact Level

Automation Risk (%)

Job Trend (Increasing / Decreasing)

## **Methodology**

**Data Cleaning & Preparation**

Standardized categorical fields

Validated numeric ranges (salary, experience, risk)

**Descriptive Analysis**

Industry-wise job openings and salary distribution

**Diagnostic Analysis**

AI impact vs automation risk comparison

Trend analysis for increasing vs decreasing roles

**Visualization**

Interactive Power BI dashboards optimized for decision-making

## **Key Insights**

Technology, Healthcare, and Finance show sustained job growth through 2030

Roles with high AI impact exhibit significantly higher automation risk

Salary dispersion is driven more by role specialization than industry alone

Job growth is concentrated in India, USA, and Australia

Declining roles are clustered in highly automatable functions
